Dossier

CLASS: Round Rogue  ·  ALIGNMENT: Chaotic Snacky

Born beneath the boardwalks of Moore Island, Jimoore never grew long — he grew round. What the other raccoons called a flaw, he calls a feature: a perfect sphere has no weak points.

Slowest walker in the arcade, fastest roller. He can't jump over a trash can, but he doesn't need to — he bounces off it, bowls through the level, and leaves with the snacks.

Moveset

TRASH ROLL[ HOLD ↓ + → ]

Tucks into a perfect sphere and accelerates downhill forever. Breaks crates, bowling-pins enemies, cannot be stopped by conventional means.

CHONK SLAM[ ↓ IN AIR ]

A short hop, then gravity does the rest. Shockwave radius scales with snacks eaten this level.

LID SHIELD[ HOLD B ]

Raises a trash-can lid. Blocks projectiles; reflects them at max CHARM.

RASCAL BOUNCE[ JUMP ON ENEMY ]

He can't jump high, but each bounce off an enemy doubles his height. Chain five for the secret "Washingtonian of the Day" fanfare.

Palette

Design Notes

Silhouette-first design: one big circle reads instantly at 16px. Head and body share the same sphere — no neck, no doubt. The striped tail is the only long element, used as a motion flag in the roll animation. Mask and belly patch give the front/back read; feet are two pixels, as nature intended.
